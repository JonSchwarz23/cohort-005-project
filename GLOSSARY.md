# Cadence

A course marketplace where learners buy and take video courses.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a Purchase, with their reason in their own words.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Chargeback, Reversal, Reversal request

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no one has decided on yet. A Purchase has at most one at a time.
_Avoid_: Pending, Pending Chargeback, requested

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request someone has accepted; it results in a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request someone has turned down; nothing is returned and access is kept.
_Avoid_: Rejected

**Refund**:
The full Amount Paid on a Purchase, returned to the learner after their Refund Request is approved. A Refund ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, cancellation

**Amount Paid**:
What the learner was actually charged for a Purchase, after any regional discount.
_Avoid_: List price, course price (when talking about refunds)

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may receive a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, under two hours watched, under half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable
